## 4. Verification Guidelines

Before accepting AI-assisted output:

1. Check factual claims against reliable sources.
2. Execute and test generated code.
3. Verify mathematical formulas and evaluation metrics.
4. Check that generated references actually exist.
5. Record corrections and limitations.
6. Do not report unverified output as an experimental finding.

## 5. Human Responsibility

All research decisions, experimental configurations, code execution,
result interpretation, and final manuscript content must be reviewed
and approved by the researcher.

AI assistance does not replace independent verification.

## 6. Update Policy

Update this document whenever significant AI assistance contributes
to a research decision, implementation, analysis, or manuscript.
